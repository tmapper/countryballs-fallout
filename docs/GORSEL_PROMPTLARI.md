## 1) menu_bg.jpg (arka plan, 16:9, en az 2560x1440)
Sol yarı boş ve karanlık kalmalı (terminal oraya geliyor), kapı sağda.

> Cinematic wide 16:9 digital painting, interior of an abandoned underground nuclear fallout shelter. On the right third of the frame, a massive round steel vault door with yellow-black hazard stripes around its frame, a large valve wheel in the center, rivets, rust and scratches, slightly ajar with warm orange light leaking from behind it. Concrete walls with exposed pipes, cables and a faded radiation warning sign, two red rotating emergency lights on the wall casting red glow, dust particles floating in the air, thin fog on the floor. The left half of the image is darker and emptier (plain concrete wall in shadow) so menu text can be placed there. Moody, dark, teal-and-orange color grading, high detail, stylized semi-realistic game key art. No text, no letters, no logos, no people, no characters.

> Video game logo on a solid pure green background (#00FF00, completely flat, no gradient, no shadow on the background). The logo reads exactly "COUNTRYBALLS" in smaller letters on top and "FALLOUT SUSPECTS" in large bold letters below. Heavy, chunky, slightly worn stencil-military typeface, off-white letters with dark outline, rusty metal texture and small scratches, a small yellow-and-black radiation trefoil symbol replacing the dot or placed between the two words, subtle orange glow behind the letters. Centered, wide horizontal layout, clean edges, no other objects, no green inside the logo.
